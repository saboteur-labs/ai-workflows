# Page template

Copy the structure between the rules below and fill in every bracket. Replace the
front matter with the project's own, if it has a convention. Leave out any section
with nothing to say.

---

```markdown
---
title: [Name the reader would look for]
summary: [One plain sentence saying what the reader can do with this page]
---

# [Name the reader would look for]

[One or two sentences: what this is and why the reader would use it.]

## [Verb-first task, such as "Set a daily goal"]

1. Open **[On-screen label]**.
2. Choose **[On-screen label]**.
3. [What to do.]

[What the reader sees when it worked, if that is not obvious.]

## [Next task]

[Steps or a short paragraph.]

## How [feature] works

[Only what helps the reader predict the product or make a decision.]

## Good to know

- [A limit or surprise, stated as what happens.] [What to do about it.]

## Related

- [Page title](relative-link.md) — [what the reader finds there]
```

---

Omit `Good to know` when there are no real limits, and `How [feature] works` when
the tasks explain themselves.
